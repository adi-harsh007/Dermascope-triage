Class reference

The seven HAM10000 lesion classes this model predicts.

Melanoma

MEL
Malignant

Malignant neoplasm of melanocytes. Requires urgent surgical excision and staging.

ABCDE criteria

Asymmetry, irregular borders, colour variation (>2 hues), diameter >6 mm, evolving.

Basal cell carcinoma

BCC
Malignant

Most common non-melanoma skin cancer. Slow growing and locally invasive; rarely metastasises.

Dermoscopic keys

Arborising telangiectatic vessels, translucent pearly papule, central ulceration.

Actinic keratosis

AKIEC
Pre-malignant

Precancerous dysplastic epidermal lesion induced by chronic solar UV exposure.

Presentation

Dry, scaly, erythematous macule or papule on sun-exposed head or extremities.

Melanocytic nevus

NV
Benign

Benign proliferation of melanocytes — common moles and birthmarks.

Benign markers

Symmetrical shape, homogeneous pigment network, sharp demarcation.

Benign keratosis

BKL
Benign

Seborrhoeic keratosis, solar lentigo, or lichen-planus-like keratosis.

Dermoscopic keys

Stuck-on warty appearance, comedo-like openings, milia-like cysts.

Dermatofibroma

DF
Benign

Benign fibrous nodule occurring primarily on the extremities of adults.

Clinical keys

Central white scar-like patch inside a delicate pigment network; positive dimple sign.

Vascular lesion

VASC
Benign

Cherry angiomas, angiokeratomas, pyogenic granulomas, or vascular malformations.

Dermoscopic keys

Red, purple or dark-blue lacunae separated by fibrous septa.

Limits

  • Measured on a held-out split of HAM10000 only. Performance on other populations, skin tones, cameras or dermatoscopes is not established by these numbers, and HAM10000 is known to under-represent darker skin.
  • The model sees one image and nothing else. No history, no clinical context, no examination, no second view of the same lesion.
  • Nothing about the lesion is measured — no size, growth, pigmentation or border metric is computed anywhere in this application.
  • This is a research decision-support tool. It is not a medical device, has no regulatory clearance, and an unflagged result is not a clinical clearance.
